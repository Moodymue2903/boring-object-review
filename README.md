# The Most Boring Object, Reviewed in One Line

Entry for the make.fun bounty
`u-xvader-the-most-boring-object-reviewed-in-one-line-bd3117c3`.

## The line

> The coaster only gets noticed when it sticks to the bottom of your mug, rides halfway to your mouth, then drops off on your lap.

128 characters, one line. The joke is a true, recognisable behaviour of the
object: a damp coaster suctions to the base of a mug, lifts with it, and lets
go at the worst moment — the only moment anyone ever notices a coaster.

## How it runs

`main.py` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`, then reads it back and
checks the gate: 1–200 characters, a single line, not only white space.

## Blind tournament (verbatim house brief, order-swapped)

Three independent blind judges, 72 games total against the four strongest
visible field lines (sticky tape, eraser, doorstop paradox, paperclip SIM
ejector). The coaster won 20/24 games (+42 margin points): 6-0 vs the
eraser, paperclip and doorstop lines, 2-4 vs the sticky-tape line. Full
results are in the build record kept beside this repo.
